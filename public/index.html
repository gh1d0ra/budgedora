        ⬇ Export CSV

        Statements: the activity download from American Express (CSV or Excel), Chase or Wells Fargo (CSV) — card or checking — or their PDF statements, or any bank's CSV / QFX / OFX. You can pick several files at once. Card payments and transfers are skipped, re-imported transactions are detected, and buckets are guessed from your history — you review everything before it's saved.
        Amazon: to see what you bought instead of “AMAZON MKTPL”, get your order history from amazon.com → Account → Request Your Information → Your Orders (Amazon emails a link, usually within a day). Pick that zip together with your card statement — or on its own to add product names to Amazon expenses already here.
        Excel: one sheet per month (named like “Aug 26”, “Sept 25”), one column per category, one amount per row; negative numbers are refunds. Summary sheets are skipped. Your file is read in your browser — you'll review everything before it's saved.
        CSV: columns date,bucket,amount,note (date as YYYY-MM-DD). Unknown buckets are created automatically.
